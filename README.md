<h1>Business Intelligence </h1>

### <p>Churn</p>

<p>Churn is the loss of clients ratio in a specific period of time. It measures how many clients avoided buying, renewing or using your service/product.</p>

<p>Why is it helpful?</p>
<ul>
  <li>Helps measuring retention and customers fidelity.</li>
  <li>Shows signs of customers dissatisfaction or loss of value.</li>
  <li>Impacts directly at revenue and growth</li>
</ul>

### <p>Cohort<p>
<p>The cohort analysis studies the behavior of a certain group of clients based on a carachteristic and a period of time. In an example, if we group clients that have bought at a store in the month of january, we can follow their behavior on subsequentially months to see if they are likely to spend more or less at that store, or if they still buy at the store or not.</p>

<p>Why is it helpful?</p>
<ul>
  <li>Identify retention patterns.</li>
  <li>Compare metrics between groups from distinct time periods.</li>
  <li>Measure campaigns, products impact.</li>
  <li>Pinpoint at what moment clients stop engaging.</li>
</ul>

<link href='https://www.optimove.com/pt/resources/learning-center/cohort-analysis'>source</link>

### <p>Lifetime Value (LTV)</p>

<p>Lifetime value is the total value that a customer has yielded for the company throughout its entire relation with the customer in question. In simpler terms, it esteem how much money a client contributes to our business from the first purchase until  the end of their lifetime with our business.</p>

<p>Why is it helpful?</p>
<ul>
  <li>Helps assess of the client acquisitions is worth.</li>
  <li>Supports marketing, sales and retention decisions.</li>
  <li>Highlights what segments are the most valueable to the company.</li>
</ul>

<link href='https://www.serasaexperian.com.br/conteudos/ltv-o-que-e-o-que-analisar/'>source</link>

### <p>Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)</p>

<p>CAC, or Customer Acquisition Cost, is how much a company spends, in average, to acquire a new customer. Usually what is taken into account is marketing costs, sales, advertisement, commercial team and other expenses related to acquire customers. This indicator helps assess if the investment to bring more clients makes sense to the business</p>

<p>Why is it helpful?</p>
<ul>
  <li>Helps manage eficiency in marketing and sales.</li>
  <li>Allows comparison between CAC with LTV to judge if the operation is sustainable.</li>
  <li>Supports decisions about channels, campaigns and comercial strategy.</li>
</ul>
