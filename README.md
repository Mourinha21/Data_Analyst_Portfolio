<h2>🛠️ Tools and Technologies</h2>

### 🐼 Pandas

<p>Pandas is one of the main libraries I use for Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA). It provides tools for creating, cleaning, transforming, and manipulating DataFrames, as well as analyzing descriptive statistics and identifying patterns within datasets.</p>

### 🧮 NumPy and SciPy

<p>NumPy and SciPy provide mathematical, statistical, and scientific computing functions. I use these libraries to perform numerical operations, calculate statistics, and apply statistical hypothesis tests to data.</p>

### 📈 Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly

<p>These libraries are used to create, customize, and display charts and graphs.

While Matplotlib and Seaborn are excellent for building detailed static visualizations, Plotly makes it possible to create interactive charts and dashboards that allow users to explore the data more dynamically.</p>

### 🤖 Scikit-learn

<p>Scikit-learn provides tools for supervised and unsupervised Machine Learning. I use it for data preprocessing, feature engineering, model selection, model training, hyperparameter tuning, and performance evaluation.</p>

### 🗄️ SQL

<p>I use SQL to retrieve, filter, aggregate, join, and manipulate data stored in relational databases. I am comfortable writing advanced queries to answer business questions and generate valuable insights.</p>

<h1>📚 Key Data Analysis Concepts</h1>

### <p>📉 Churn Rate</p>

<p>Churn rate represents the percentage of customers who stop purchasing, renewing, or using a company’s product or service during a specific period.</p>

<p>Why is it helpful?</p>
<ul>
  <li>Helps measuring retention and customers fidelity.</li>
  <li>Shows signs of customers dissatisfaction or loss of value.</li>
  <li>Impacts directly at revenue and growth</li>
</ul>

[source](https://www.totvs.com/blog/inteligencia-dados/churn/)

### <p>👥 Cohort Analysis<p>
<p>Cohort analysis studies the behavior of a specific group of customers who share a common characteristic or experience during a particular period.

For example, customers who made their first purchase in January can be grouped into a cohort. Their behavior can then be tracked over the following months to determine whether they continue purchasing, spend more or less over time, or eventually stop engaging with the business.</p>

<p>Why is it helpful?</p>
<ul>
  <li>Identify retention patterns.</li>
  <li>Compare metrics between groups from distinct time periods.</li>
  <li>Measure campaigns, products impact.</li>
  <li>Pinpoint at what moment clients stop engaging.</li>
</ul>

[source](https://www.optimove.com/pt/resources/learning-center/cohort-analysis/)

### <p>Customer Lifetime Value — LTV</p>

<p>Customer Lifetime Value, or LTV, estimates the total revenue a customer is expected to generate throughout their entire relationship with a company.

In simpler terms, it estimates how much financial value a customer contributes to the business—from their first purchase until the end of their relationship with the company.</p>

<p>Why is it helpful?</p>
<ul>
  <li>Helps assess of the client acquisitions is worth.</li>
  <li>Supports marketing, sales and retention decisions.</li>
  <li>Highlights what segments are the most valueable to the company.</li>
</ul>

[source](https://www.serasaexperian.com.br/conteudos/ltv-o-que-e-o-que-analisar/)

### <p>🎯 Customer Acquisition Cost — CAC</p>

<p>Customer Acquisition Cost, or CAC, represents the average amount a company spends to acquire a new customer.

The calculation usually includes expenses related to marketing, advertising, sales teams, campaigns, commissions, and other customer acquisition activities.</p>

<p>Why is it helpful?</p>
<ul>
  <li>Helps manage eficiency in marketing and sales.</li>
  <li>Allows comparison between CAC with LTV to judge if the operation is sustainable.</li>
  <li>Supports decisions about channels, campaigns and comercial strategy.</li>
</ul>

[source](https://sebraeplay.com.br/content/custo-de-aquisicao-do-cliente-cac)

### 📌 Key Performance Indicators — KPIs

<p>Key Performance Indicators, or KPIs, are measurable values used to determine whether a business, team, project, or process is progressing toward a specific goal.</p>

<p>Why is it helpful?</p>
<ul>
  <li>Tracks progress towards goals.</li>
  <li>Helps data driven decisions.</li>
  <li>Prioritizes what's most impactful towards the result.</li>
</ul>

[source](https://www.tableau.com/pt-br/learn/articles/what-is-KPI)
